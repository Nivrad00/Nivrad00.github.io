THE STORY SO FAR

Evenfall is known as the city of eternal sunset—an ex-mining colony where the sun hangs motionless below the horizon. One day, all of its outgoing communication goes dark.

Intrepid technician Indigo is sent to diagnose the issue. The first thing they discover? Evenfall is teeming with robots. Not that they're complaining; they've always preferred the company of machines over their fellow humans.

But with megacorporate support dwindling, Evenfall is on the brink of collapse. As Indigo digs into the city's secrets, they uncover a conspiracy that might finally spell an end to the twilit metropolis—with Indigo caught in the middle.

THERE ARE CUTE ROBOTS!

Meet the characters carving out a life at the far end of the galaxy...

TAZ

If you run across this charming loner in a bar, be careful of his silver tongue—he's just as likely to swipe your chits as he is to take you home.

EM

As one of the last employees in Evenfall's gutted Department of Information, Em knows to keep their head down. But when the streetlights turn off, she dreams of chronicling the city's complex history.

SHONKHOR

Abandoned by his multibillionaire owner, Shonkhor is in search of a new directive to follow. But can he find someone to fill the human-shaped hole in his heart?

ABOUT THE DEVELOPER

Hi, I'm Niv. I'm a creator deer who makes video games about friendship and exploration. I also have a soft spot for first-person shooters, rhythm games, and puzzle games. You can play my previous game Purrgatory on itch.io or Steam.

I've been creating for a long time, but I'm new to making money off of it. Currently I work a day job at a library to support myself and my partner. With your support, I hope to one day make a living off of creating games like this. Thank you for helping make my dreams a reality!

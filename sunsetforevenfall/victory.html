ABOUT EVENFALL

Evenfall is known as the city of eternal sunset—a remote mining colony where the sun hangs motionless just below the horizon. Months ago, all of the planet's outgoing communication went dark.

When intrepid technician Indigo is sent to diagnose the issue, they discover more than they ever signed up for. The first surprise? There are no humans. Indigo finds themself surrounded by artificial life, from administrators delivering programmed platitudes to rabble conserving energy in the city's underbelly.

Furthermore, between rusty skywalks and cost-cutting megacorps, Evenfall is on the brink of collapse. As Indigo digs into the city's secrets, they uncover a conspiracy that might finally spell an end to the twilit metropolis—with Indigo caught in the middle of it.

Step into Indigo's shoes and explore a hand-drawn point-and-click world filled with robots of all shapes and sizes.

Get to know the robots trying to carve out a life for themselves in a dying city:

Alias: Taz

If you run across this charming loner in a bar, be careful of his silver tongue--he's just as likely to swipe your credit chit as he is to take you home.

Alias: Em

As one of the last employees in Evenfall's gutted Department of Information, Em knows to keep their head down. But when the streetlights turn off, they dream of chronicling the city's complex history.

Alias: Shonkhor

Abandoned by his multibillionaire owner, Shonkhor is in search of a new directive to follow--and someone to fill the human-shaped hole in his heart.

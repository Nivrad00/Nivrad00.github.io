████████ is a former mining hub and the only populated city on LXC-2152b. Because of its location in LXC-2152b's tidally locked habitable ring, the sun always remains just below the horizon, casting the city in eternal sunset or sunrise.

Legacy

With off-planet funding diminishing by the year, ████████ is a ghost city with many struggling to survive on its dimly lit streets. Some historians have called the city a house of cards—a project born of hubris, destined to fall under its own weight[2]. After all, analysts have warned for over a century that the cost of FTL communication and travel would be impractical for extra-remote settlements[6] such as ████████. Megacorps invested in ████████ anyway, many under the misguided assumption that technological development would continue to grow exponentially, and that the logistical challenges posed by Far Sagittarius would be erased by tomorrow’s inventors and engineers. When such a miracle solution failed to appear, megacorps cut their losses and moved to new ventures, leaving ████████ and its many robotic residents to live with endless oil leaks and flickering lights. Though LumiTrust provides just enough funding to keep ████████ afloat, only a thin web of contracts and favors ensures the megacorp's continued presence on LXC-2152b, leaving it uncertain when the house of cards will finally fall.

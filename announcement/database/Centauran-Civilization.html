Centauran Civilization

Article Discussion

The Centauran Civilization is an ancient civilization thought to have inhabited the Centaurus Arm of the Milky Way, millions of years before humans evolved on Earth.

Discovery

In 2190, a geological study on the agricultural planet Kepler-62 discovered an unnatural, fractal-like distribution[9] of minerals in the planet's crust. Some experts believed this to be signs of ancient terraforming, which would imply the existence of an alien civilization.

Experts were in disagreement about whether this constituted proof of alien life until 2213, when seemingly alien-made artifacts were discovered on the nearby Kepler-14. These artifacts, made out of a durable carbon-based polymer, contained fractal-like quantum geometries that were more complex than previously thought possible. They are sometimes described as an advanced form of today's nanomaterials[13]. Similarities between Kepler-62's mineral deposits and Kepler-14's artifacts led researchers to hypothesize that they shared an origin, which was dubbed the Centauran Civilization.

Search for artifacts

The Kepler discoveries prompted a frenzied race between corporations to discover the next alien artifact. So far, Centauran artifacts have been discovered at two other sites, claimed by companies NoTo and Jinbu respectively.

LumiTrust participates in this archeological search through its subsidiary LumiTrust Speculative. Though LumiTrust has yet to discover any artifacts, it has identified planets with potential signs of Centauran terraforming, such as the remote LXC-2152b.

The Pan-Galactic Trade Organization, who governs the original Kepler-14 site, has attempted to designate alien evidence as a common good of humanity. However, this legislation has yet to be ratified, meaning corporations who discover Centauran artifacts can still declare them proprietary[7] and sell them on the private market. Individual artifacts have been auctioned at prices reaching 80 billion USD.

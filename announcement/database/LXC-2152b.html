LXC-2152b is a tidally locked planet orbiting the star LXC-2152 in the Far Sagittarius region. Its only populated city, ████████, is one of the most remote settlements in the Milky Way. The planet is 7 kilolight-years from New Gujarat, the nearest populated stellar system, and 54 kilolight-years from Earth.

Physical characteristics

LXC-21512b is an Earth-like rocky planet with a radius of 1.03 R🜨 and a mass estimated to be 1.96 M[E]. Its crust contains valuable deposits of nickel, iron, rare-earth metals, and platinum. It orbits its star at a distance of about 0.32 AU with an orbital period of roughly 133.4 days.

As a tidally locked planet, its rotation is synchronized with its orbit, meaning half of the planet always faces the star and half always faces away. This results in extreme temperatures estimated to reach 330 °C (626 °F) on the starward side and -180 °C (-292 °F) on the leeward side. A habitable ring between the planet’s hot and cold hemispheres presents conditions most favorable for terraforming and life support, and is the location of the planet’s only major settlement, ████████.

History

Pre-human civilization

Geological analysis of LXC-2152b has revealed fractal patterns similar to those associated with the pre-human Centauran Civilization, implying there may have been intelligent life on the planet before human discovery. However, no alien artifacts have been found on the planet to date, despite extensive efforts[3].

Discovery and Second Galactic Rush

LXC-2152b was discovered and catalogued in 2031, during NASA’s Far Reaches mission.

In the mid-2080’s during the Second Galactic Rush, investigations led primarily by StarPAC and LumiTrust confirmed significant mineralization on LXC-2152b, causing the planet to be added to the Far Sagittarius Distribution Plan (FSDP). Over a dozen land claims on LXC-2152b were recognized[5] by the FSDP.

Early settlement

StarPAC achieved first landing on LXC-2152b on 2096, with multiple mining bases operational within years. LumiTrust was one of the first megacorps on-planet, with its two largest bases being Avaoga Valley and Atacama. LumiTrust’s yield from LXC-2152b was approximately 50% nickel, 34% iron, 10% rare-earth metals, 5% platinum, and 1% other minerals.

In 2101, LumiTrust oversaw the construction of many of the planet’s reconfigurable manufacturies, as well as the city of ████████. Though originally intended as a hub for mine workers, ████████’s favorable climate, economic prosperity, and lack of regulation soon attracted prison colonies, refugees, entrepreneurs, and adventure-seekers alike. For more information, see History of ████████.

Decline

Over the next half century, LXC-2152b’s mineral resources were fully extracted, and mines started to shut down. In the 2160’s, megacorporations began withdrawing from the colony, as its remoteness made it too expensive to maintain without profits from mining. As a direct result, ████████’s population started plummeting. By 2180, LumiTrust was the last remaining corporation with a presence on the planet, and there were less than 1000 humans. The robot population remained high, as the cost to transport service automata off of LXC-2152b was often higher than the cost of their manufacture[4].

Human recall

In February of 2220, LumiTrust recalled all humans from ████████, requiring them to find alternate arrangements on less-remote colonies. Today, only robots remain on the planet.

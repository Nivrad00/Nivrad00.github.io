Second-gen FTL

Article Discussion

Subparticle dual-acceleration faster-than-light technology, known commonly as second-gen FTL, is a type of faster-than-light engine first developed in the 2070's.

Although second-gen FTL's capabilities increased quickly during the first few years of development[11], it has quickly reached a cap around 200 Kc for space travel and 20 Mc for photon-encoded communication. It takes about five months to travel from Earth to Far Sagittarius, the furthest region of the Milky Way with permanent residents[1].
